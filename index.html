The PAUSE approach

Designed around your control.

Good security software should be clear about what it knows, what it stores and where its limits begin.

01 / LOCAL-FIRST

Your device, your workspace

Core checks and activity records are designed to run locally. Optional connected services are disclosed where they apply.

02 / EXPLAINABLE

Clarity over false certainty

Results are based on configured checks and heuristics. Unknown means unknown — not secretly safe.

03 / DELIBERATE

Actions stay intentional

PAUSE keeps supported actions reviewable and avoids claiming universal interception or invisible protection.

A note on protection: PAUSE is an evolving development project, not a certified antivirus, firewall or endpoint security product, and it has not been independently security-audited. Keep your operating system protection and browser Safe Browsing enabled. A SAFE result is not a guarantee that a file, link or message is harmless.
